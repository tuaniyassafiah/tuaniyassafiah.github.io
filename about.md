# About Me

Hello! Welcome to my personal learning blog.

I am a student of Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka, currently learning about Cloud Manufacturing and its related technologies.

This blog serves as a platform to document my learning journey, share knowledge, and explore how emerging technologies contribute to modern manufacturing industries.

## My Learning Goals

- Understand the concept of Cloud Manufacturing.
- Explore the role of Internet of Things (IoT) in manufacturing.
- Learn about cloud computing and enabling technologies.
- Understand the applications of smart manufacturing in industry.

## About This Blog

This blog is created as part of my Cloud Manufacturing course. It contains learning notes, discussions, reflections, and information related to the topics covered throughout the semester.
