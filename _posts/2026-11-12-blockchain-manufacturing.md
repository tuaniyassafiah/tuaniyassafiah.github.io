## 1. Introduction

Blockchain technology is a distributed digital ledger that records transactions securely and transparently across a network. Information stored in a blockchain is organised into blocks that are connected using cryptographic techniques. Once information has been recorded and validated, it becomes difficult to modify without agreement from the network.

In manufacturing, blockchain technology can improve information sharing, product traceability, supply chain transparency, and data security. It allows manufacturers, suppliers, distributors, and customers to access verified information about products and manufacturing activities.

## 2. How Blockchain Technology Works

Blockchain operates through several important processes:

1. **Transaction initiation:** A participant submits information or a transaction to the blockchain network.
2. **Transaction validation:** Network participants or authorised systems verify the submitted information.
3. **Block creation:** Validated transactions are grouped into a block.
4. **Block linking:** The new block is connected to previous blocks through cryptographic hashes.
5. **Record distribution:** The updated ledger is shared across the participating network.

This process creates a shared record that can improve accountability and reduce unauthorised changes to information.

## 3. Applications in Manufacturing

Blockchain can support several manufacturing activities:

- **Product traceability:** Records product movement and manufacturing history from raw materials to finished goods.
- **Supply chain management:** Improves information sharing between suppliers, manufacturers, and distributors.
- **Quality assurance:** Stores inspection results, production records, and quality certifications.
- **Inventory management:** Provides more reliable information about product availability and movement.
- **Smart contracts:** Automates selected business agreements when predefined conditions are fulfilled.

## 4. Benefits of Blockchain Technology

Blockchain offers several potential benefits to manufacturing organisations. Its distributed structure improves transparency because authorised participants can access consistent transaction records. Product traceability also becomes more effective because manufacturing and supply chain information can be recorded throughout the product lifecycle.

Furthermore, blockchain can reduce information discrepancies between organisations, improve accountability, and support the verification of product authenticity.

## 5. Challenges and Limitations

Despite its advantages, blockchain implementation involves several challenges. These include high implementation costs, integration with existing manufacturing systems, scalability limitations, energy consumption in certain blockchain networks, and the need for technical expertise.

Data privacy is another important consideration. Organisations must carefully determine which information can be shared and which information must remain confidential.

## 6. Conclusion

Blockchain technology has the potential to improve transparency, traceability, and information security in manufacturing and supply chain management. Although implementation challenges remain, its integration with other Industry 4.0 technologies can support more connected, reliable, and intelligent manufacturing operations.
