## 1. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the important technologies supporting the development of smart manufacturing. AI refers to the ability of computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, analysing information, recognising patterns and making decisions.

In modern manufacturing, AI can be integrated with machines, sensors, robotics and digital systems to improve production activities. It allows manufacturers to analyse operational data, identify potential problems and support more efficient manufacturing processes.

According to Gao et al. (2024), AI applications in manufacturing include production planning, process optimisation, quality assurance, maintenance and automated assembly. Therefore, AI plays an important role in the development of intelligent and data-driven manufacturing systems.

## 2. How Artificial Intelligence Works in Manufacturing

AI systems use data and computational methods to identify patterns, generate predictions and support decisions.

The general process involves several steps:

1. **Data Collection:** Information is collected from sensors, machines, production systems and other sources.
2. **Data Processing:** The collected data is organised and prepared for analysis.
3. **Data Analysis:** AI algorithms analyse the information to identify patterns, relationships and unusual conditions.
4. **Prediction or Decision Support:** The system generates predictions or recommendations based on the analysis.
5. **Action and Monitoring:** Operators or automated systems use the results to support manufacturing activities and monitor performance.

The accuracy and usefulness of AI results depend on the quality of the data and the suitability of the AI methods used.

## 3. Applications of AI in Smart Manufacturing

AI can be applied to several important manufacturing activities.

**3.1 Predictive Maintenance**

AI can analyse machine operating data, including temperature, vibration and performance records, to identify patterns associated with equipment problems. This helps manufacturers predict possible failures and plan maintenance activities before unexpected breakdowns occur.

**3.2 Quality Inspection**

AI-based inspection systems can analyse product images and production data to identify defects or irregularities. This supports quality control and helps manufacturers detect problems during production.

**3.3 Production Planning and Scheduling**

AI can support production planning by analysing production requirements, available resources, machine capacity and scheduling constraints. This helps manufacturers organise production activities more effectively.

**3.4 Process Optimisation**

AI can analyse manufacturing process data to identify opportunities for improving operating parameters and production performance. This may help reduce material waste, improve efficiency and maintain product quality.

**3.5 Robotics and Automation**

AI can support robotic systems in activities such as product handling, assembly and inspection. Intelligent robotic systems can use information from their environment to support task execution and interaction with production equipment.

**3.6 Supply Chain Management**

AI can analyse information related to inventory, customer demand, suppliers and product delivery. This supports demand forecasting, inventory planning and supply chain decision-making.

## 4. Types of AI Technologies Used in Manufacturing

Several AI-related technologies can support manufacturing operations.

**4.1 Machine Learning (ML)**

Machine Learning is a branch of AI that allows computer systems to learn patterns from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed for every individual task. In manufacturing, ML can be used for predictive maintenance, quality inspection and production analysis.

**4.2 Deep Learning**

Deep Learning is a type of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers. It can be applied to complex tasks such as image recognition, defect detection and analysis of machine operating data.

**4.3 Computer Vision**

Computer Vision enables computer systems to interpret and analyse visual information such as images and videos. In manufacturing, it can support automated product inspection, defect detection and robotic guidance.

**4.4 Intelligent Decision Support**

AI-based decision support systems analyse available information and provide recommendations to assist managers and operators in making manufacturing decisions.

## 5. Benefits of AI in Manufacturing

The implementation of AI can provide several benefits:

- **Improved productivity:** AI supports the analysis and optimisation of manufacturing activities.
- **Better product quality:** Automated inspection can help identify product defects and irregularities.
- **Reduced downtime:** Predictive maintenance helps identify possible equipment problems earlier.
- **Improved decision-making:** AI provides data-based predictions and recommendations.
- **Efficient resource utilisation:** AI can support the planning and use of machines, materials and production resources.
- **Greater automation:** AI can support intelligent robotic systems and automated manufacturing processes.

## 6. Challenges of Implementing AI

Despite its advantages, manufacturers may face several challenges when implementing AI technologies.

**6.1 Data Quality and Availability**

AI systems require suitable data for training, testing and operation. Incomplete, inaccurate or insufficient data may affect the reliability of AI predictions.

**6.2 High Implementation Costs**

Implementing AI may require investment in sensors, computing infrastructure, software and specialised technical resources.

**6.3 System Integration**

Integrating AI technologies with existing machines, software and control systems can be difficult, especially when different systems use different standards and technologies.

**6.4 Cybersecurity Risks**

Connected AI systems may be exposed to cybersecurity threats. Manufacturers need suitable security measures to protect operational data and production systems.

**6.5 Lack of Technical Skills**

Employees may require additional training and knowledge to operate, maintain and understand AI-based manufacturing systems.

**6.6 Reliability and Explainability**

Manufacturers need to understand how AI systems produce their results. In important production activities, unclear or unreliable recommendations may create operational and safety concerns. Therefore, AI systems should be properly evaluated and monitored by qualified personnel.

## 7. Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence is an important technology in smart manufacturing because it enables computer systems to analyse data, recognise patterns, generate predictions and support decision-making. Its applications include predictive maintenance, quality inspection, production planning, process optimisation, robotics and supply chain management.

Although AI offers opportunities to improve productivity, product quality and operational efficiency, manufacturers must consider challenges related to data quality, implementation costs, system integration and system reliability. With proper implementation and human supervision, AI can contribute significantly to the development of intelligent and efficient manufacturing environments.
