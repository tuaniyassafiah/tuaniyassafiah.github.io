## 1. Introduction

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN) are an important technology related to the Internet of Things (IoT). A WSN consists of multiple sensor nodes that are distributed across an area to collect information about physical or environmental conditions. These nodes communicate wirelessly to transfer the collected information to a central system for further processing and analysis.

In manufacturing, WSN can support the monitoring of equipment and production environments without requiring extensive wired connections.

## 2. Main Components of a Wireless Sensor Network

A typical wireless sensor network consists of several important components.

### 2.1 Sensor Nodes

Sensor nodes are devices that collect information from their surroundings. They may measure different conditions such as temperature, humidity, pressure, vibration and movement.

### 2.2 Processing Unit

The processing unit manages the collected information and performs basic data processing before the information is transmitted.

### 2.3 Wireless Communication

Wireless communication allows sensor nodes to transmit information to other nodes or a central collection point without physical cables.

### 2.4 Gateway

A gateway connects the wireless sensor network to another network or an Internet-based system. It allows collected sensor information to be transferred for further analysis and monitoring.

## 3. How Wireless Sensor Networks Work

The operation of a WSN generally involves several steps:

1. **Data collection:** Sensor nodes detect physical or environmental conditions.
2. **Data processing:** The collected information is processed by the sensor node or another processing device.
3. **Wireless transmission:** Information is transmitted through wireless communication.
4. **Data collection at the gateway:** A gateway receives and forwards information from the sensor network.
5. **Monitoring and analysis:** The information can be accessed through a connected system for monitoring and decision-making.

This process allows information from different locations to be collected and monitored efficiently.

## 4. Applications of WSN in Manufacturing

Wireless sensor networks can support different manufacturing activities.

### 4.1 Machine Condition Monitoring

Sensors can monitor machine conditions such as temperature and vibration. This information can help identify unusual operating conditions and support maintenance activities.

### 4.2 Environmental Monitoring

WSN can monitor environmental conditions within manufacturing facilities, including temperature and humidity. This is useful when environmental conditions need to be maintained within suitable operating limits.

### 4.3 Production Monitoring

Wireless sensor nodes can collect information from different areas of a production facility. This information can help manufacturers understand operating conditions and monitor production activities.

### 4.4 Equipment Monitoring

Sensor networks can support the monitoring of equipment distributed across a facility. The collected information can help operators identify possible problems and make appropriate decisions.

## 5. Benefits and Challenges of WSN

### 5.1 Benefits

Some benefits of wireless sensor networks include:

- **Flexible installation:** Wireless communication reduces the need for extensive physical cabling.
- **Continuous monitoring:** Sensor nodes can collect information at regular intervals.
- **Wide-area monitoring:** Multiple sensor nodes can monitor different locations.
- **Improved decision-making:** Collected information can help operators understand equipment and environmental conditions.

### 5.2 Challenges

Despite their benefits, WSN also have several challenges:

- **Power limitations:** Many sensor nodes depend on batteries and require efficient energy management.
- **Communication reliability:** Wireless signals may be affected by interference and physical obstacles.
- **Security:** Information transmitted wirelessly needs appropriate protection.
- **Maintenance:** Sensor nodes require monitoring and maintenance to ensure reliable operation.

## 6. Conclusion

Wireless Sensor Networks are an important technology that supports IoT by connecting distributed sensor nodes through wireless communication. They allow physical and environmental information to be collected and transmitted for monitoring and analysis. In manufacturing, WSN can support machine condition monitoring, environmental monitoring and equipment management. Therefore, WSN can contribute to more connected and information-driven manufacturing operations.
