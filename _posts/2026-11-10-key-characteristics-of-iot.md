## 1.0 Introduction

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to a connected environment in which different objects and devices communicate and exchange information through networks. IoT enables physical objects to become part of a connected system, allowing information to be collected and shared.

IoT is associated with several important characteristics that explain how connected devices operate and interact. Understanding these characteristics helps us understand the role of IoT in modern industries and manufacturing environments.

## 2.0 Key Characteristics of IoT

### 2.1 Ubiquitous

Ubiquitous means that IoT connectivity and services can be available in many places and situations. Connected devices can provide access to information beyond a single location.

### 2.2 Pervasive

Pervasive refers to the presence of connected technology within different environments. IoT devices can be integrated into everyday objects and industrial equipment.

### 2.3 Wireless

Wireless communication allows IoT devices to exchange information without requiring a physical cable connection. This supports flexibility when connecting devices in different locations.

### 2.4 Mobile

Mobile refers to the ability of connected devices or systems to operate while moving between locations. This characteristic supports applications that require continuous connectivity.

### 2.5 Wearable

Wearable devices are electronic devices designed to be worn by users. They can be connected to networks to collect or exchange information.

### 2.6 Distributed

Distributed means that IoT devices and functions can be located across different physical locations rather than being concentrated in one place.

### 2.7 Embedded

Embedded technology refers to computing capabilities integrated into physical devices or equipment. It allows objects to perform specific functions and interact with other connected systems.

### 2.8 Dynamic

Dynamic refers to the ability of an IoT environment to respond to changing conditions. Connected devices may operate and exchange information as their surrounding conditions change.

### 2.9 Energy-Aware

Energy-aware refers to the consideration of energy consumption when designing and operating IoT devices. Efficient energy usage is important, particularly for devices that depend on batteries.

## 3.0 Importance of IoT Characteristics in Manufacturing

These characteristics support the development of connected manufacturing environments.

For example, wireless communication can reduce the need for extensive physical cabling when connecting equipment. Distributed devices can collect information from different production areas, while embedded technology allows machines and equipment to perform specific monitoring functions.

In addition, dynamic systems can respond to changing operating conditions, while energy-aware design helps support the operation of devices that depend on limited power sources.

Together, these characteristics contribute to the development of more connected and flexible manufacturing operations.

## 4.0 Conclusion

In conclusion, IoT has several important characteristics, including ubiquitous, pervasive, wireless, mobile, wearable, distributed, embedded, dynamic, and energy-aware. These characteristics explain how IoT devices and systems can operate within connected environments.

Understanding these characteristics provides a foundation for learning how IoT technologies can be applied in manufacturing industries and other industrial applications.
