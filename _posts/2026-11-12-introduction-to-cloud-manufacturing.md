## 1. Introduction

The manufacturing industry is continuously evolving due to the development of digital technologies. Traditional manufacturing systems often depend on physical equipment, local computing resources, and individual production facilities. Although these systems support production activities, they may involve high investment costs, limited resource flexibility, and difficulties in sharing manufacturing resources.

Cloud Manufacturing (CMfg) is an emerging manufacturing model that combines cloud computing technologies with manufacturing processes. It allows manufacturing resources, services, and capabilities to be accessed through networks when required. This approach supports resource sharing, flexible production, and improved collaboration between manufacturing organisations.

## 2. Definition of Cloud Manufacturing

Cloud Manufacturing is a service-oriented manufacturing model that provides access to distributed manufacturing resources and capabilities through a cloud-based platform.

These resources may include:

- Manufacturing machines and equipment.
- Production facilities and factories.
- Design and engineering software.
- Manufacturing information and databases.
- Production planning and scheduling services.
- Testing and quality inspection facilities.

Instead of owning and managing every manufacturing resource independently, organisations can access selected services based on their production requirements.

## 3. How Cloud Manufacturing Works

Cloud Manufacturing operates by connecting manufacturing resources and service providers through a shared digital platform.

The general process involves the following steps:

1. **Resource connection:** Manufacturing equipment, software, and facilities are connected to the network.
2. **Resource virtualisation:** Physical manufacturing resources are represented as digital resources that can be managed through the platform.
3. **Service publication:** Available manufacturing capabilities are presented as services.
4. **Service selection:** Customers or manufacturers identify services that match their requirements.
5. **Task execution:** Selected resources perform the required manufacturing activities.
6. **Monitoring and feedback:** Production performance and service information are monitored to support continuous improvement.

This process enables manufacturing resources to be accessed and coordinated more efficiently.

## 4. Key Technologies Supporting Cloud Manufacturing

Cloud Manufacturing is supported by several digital technologies.

### 4.1 Cloud Computing

Cloud computing provides computing resources, storage, and software services through the internet. It allows manufacturers to access digital resources without depending entirely on local computing infrastructure.

### 4.2 Internet of Things (IoT)

IoT connects machines, sensors, and devices to collect and exchange manufacturing data. It supports real-time monitoring of equipment conditions and production activities.

### 4.3 Big Data Analytics

Big data analytics processes large amounts of manufacturing information to identify patterns, evaluate performance, and support better decision-making.

### 4.4 Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI can analyse production data, support predictive maintenance, optimise scheduling, and improve manufacturing decisions.

### 4.5 Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS)

CPS integrates physical manufacturing processes with computational and communication systems. It enables interaction between physical equipment and digital monitoring or control systems.

## 5. Applications of Cloud Manufacturing

Cloud Manufacturing can be applied in various manufacturing activities, including:

- **Product design:** Sharing design software and engineering resources through cloud platforms.
- **Production planning:** Coordinating manufacturing schedules and resource allocation.
- **Remote monitoring:** Monitoring equipment and production performance from different locations.
- **Collaborative manufacturing:** Connecting multiple manufacturers and service providers.
- **Maintenance management:** Sharing equipment information and supporting maintenance planning.

## 6. Conclusion

Cloud Manufacturing represents an important development in modern manufacturing by integrating cloud computing with distributed manufacturing resources and services. It provides opportunities for manufacturers to share resources, improve production flexibility, and enhance collaboration. Through the integration of technologies such as IoT, big data analytics, AI, and cyber-physical systems, Cloud Manufacturing can support the development of more connected and intelligent manufacturing environments.
